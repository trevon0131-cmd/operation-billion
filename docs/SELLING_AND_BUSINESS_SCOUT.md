# Seller operations & local-business research

## Storefront operations

The stage `NOT GUILTY` authorizes offline creation only. The Seller Operations
Manager produces metadata, delivery zip, suggested pricing, listing draft and
unsent replies. A store connection will require a real account, identity and
payout verification, seller-category rights, pricing limits, terms agreement,
and a platform-supported API. **Nothing here establishes a seller account.**

## Different ideas after guilty or unsuccessful trials

Court rejects all restricted actions, records reasons and creates separately
judged lawful alternatives where a known option exists. If after 14 days a
published experiment shows no positive reported net earnings, it retires, and
selects a specialist in a different business lane. At day 7 an offer with at
least 100 reported views and no clicks can retire early. Retiring a local draft
as UNLAUNCHED does not prove a market failed. Cases are never deleted. If the
bounded catalog is exhausted, it stops and requests new ideas rather than
claiming to have invented new products that cannot be built.

## Google Maps and B2B

Google Maps Platform terms prohibit scraping/exporting its content outside
specified uses. We deliberately do not embed a Google Maps scraper, auto
harvest emails, or evade CAPTCHAs. Business research can instead use permitted
source lists, consenting leads, and publicly available company websites. The
initial version handles a single user-specified public HTTPS business site,
obeys robots.txt, and produces a page-format observation report only.

No unsolicited emails are sent. A future integration can prepare personalized,
accurate outreach for human review and comply with applicable laws and service
terms, but it cannot claim an existing client relationship.
