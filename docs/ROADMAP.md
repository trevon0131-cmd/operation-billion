# Scale roadmap (not a promise)

Goal dashboard target: $1,000,000,000 gross cumulative revenue as an *aspiration only*. Even $100 of verified profit would be a meaningful first test; no assumption of progression from $0 to $1 billion is justified.

## Gates

- **Gate A: program correctness:** unit tests, no writing outside owned output folders, no auto-spend, auditable court, secure local server.
- **Gate B: product quality:** manual review of every artwork/template; typography, print resolution, file formats, actual usability, licensing and accessibility.
- **Gate C: legitimate listing and delivery:** storefront account accepted, terms reviewed, actual deliverable available; make your first manual listing.
- **Gate D: real demand:** sourced impressions, conversion data, returns, refunds, complaints, actual profit after platform, production, and transaction costs.
- **Gate E: controlled automation:** official API-backed read-only account metrics, then *only authorized draft creation*; do not introduce auto-transactions just for growth.

## Features deliberately not in v0.1

A thousand concurrent selling bots, full automatic storefront creation, banking, tax filings, market guarantees, independent self-changing code, auto-negotiation with strangers, external AI judge with purchasing rights, scraping sites against policies, automated account signup, or unsupported eBay digital-product listings.

Reason: each feature adds account, risk, money or policy obligations, and zero cost cannot remove the cost of physical inventory/shipping or marketplace fees.
